Suggestions that show their working

Every quantity and date comes with the reasons behind it, in plain words and numbers, down to the source records. A planner can trust a suggestion or argue with it. One the system cannot explain is treated as a bug.

§ 01 · What it does

Suggestions in practice

  • 01

    Netting that adds up

    On hand, open purchases, transfers and production, against sales, forecast, component needs and transfer demand. Forecast and orders are consumed against each other so demand is not counted twice.

  • 02

    Policies to suit the item

    Min and max, reorder point, periodic review, economic order quantity, lot for lot and make to order. Safety stock from a service level, or as weeks of forward cover.

  • 03

    Transfer first

    Spare stock at another location is used before anything new is bought. Transfer-replenished items buy at the source using the source's buying rules.

  • 04

    Vendor order building

    Minimum order value, order multiples, price breaks, freight-free thresholds and order days. Orders are consolidated per vendor, and container fill is shown.

  • 05

    Calm plans

    Frozen, Firm and Open zones from the time fences, plus tolerances, so a delivery moving a day or two does not set off a flood of messages.

  • 06

    Shortages stay visible

    Projected stock is never floored at zero. A shortage shows with its date and quantity.

  • 07

    Exceptions, not noise

    Stockout risk, excess, dead stock, late supply, below vendor minimum and a gap between plan and history.

§ 02 · In a real situation

Why this suggestion says buy 50

Worked example, illustrative

An item has 130 on hand and a plan of 30 a week. Safety stock is three weeks of cover, so 90. The lead time is two weeks and the vendor sells in multiples of 50.

Without a new order, projected stock is 100 at the end of week 1, 70 at the end of week 2 and 40 at the end of week 3. An order placed today arrives at the start of week 3, so it cannot help week 2. The explanation says so, and flags that safety stock is breached in week 2.

To end week 3 at the safety stock of 90 the item needs 50 more, because 90 minus 40 is 50. That is already a multiple of 50, so the suggestion is to buy 50. Each line of that reasoning is a step the planner can open and check.
