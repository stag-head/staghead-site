§ 01 · What it does

Setup in practice

  • 01

    Setup wizard

    Six steps from Assisted Setup. Leave part way and come back, and your progress is kept. It finishes by opening the health check.

  • 02

    Health check

    Lists what is missing or broken, such as a nightly feed that is not scheduled or an item mapping that points at a blocked item, and offers a Fix action.

  • 03

    Choose what to plan

    Add items by filter, for example one item category, and mark them as planned. Run it again and nothing is duplicated.

  • 04

    Planning calendar

    Uses the base calendar on Company Information, so non-working days such as Good Friday are respected when weeks and months are split.

  • 05

    Roles that match the job

    Separate permission sets for the administrator, the demand planner and a read-only viewer. A viewer sees every page but cannot change anything.

  • 06

    Tooltips and a manual

    Each field has a tooltip and its own entry in the manual, with a worked example of the effect of changing it.
