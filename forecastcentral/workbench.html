Part 06 · Planned

A workbench for the daily job

Buyers and planners work through queues, check the reasons, change what needs changing and create the orders. All of it happens in Business Central, and the orders it creates are the standard ones.

RolesBuyer, production planner, demand planner
EditionAll Business Central editions
StatusPlanned
§ 01 · What it does

Workbench in practice

  • 01

    Queues by urgency

    By vendor for purchases, by location for transfers, and by work centre or family for production, with the most urgent first.

  • 02

    Edit where you look

    Change a planning parameter or a suggested quantity or date on the line, and that item recalculates straight away.

  • 03

    Accept, change or reject

    Rejections and changes carry a reason, and overrides are tracked, so the numbers can be tuned later.

  • 04

    Standard documents

    Creating orders produces ordinary purchase, transfer and production orders, and your usual approvals apply.

  • 05

    The item planning card

    History, plan and projected stock on one page. Projected stock shows two lines, confirmed supply only and including proposals, against safety stock, with weeks of cover and the supply gap.

  • 06

    Planner proposals persist

    A suggestion a planner has edited or confirmed is kept as a firm proposal and is not overwritten by the next run.

§ 02 · In a real situation

Monday with the buyer

Example

The buyer opens the vendor queue. The first vendor is 400 under its minimum order value, and the workbench says so. The buyer opens one line, reads why it was suggested, brings a quantity forward to top up the order, and sees the projected stock redraw before saving.

One click creates the purchase order, which then goes through the normal approval.
