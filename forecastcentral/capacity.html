Part 07 · Planned

Capacity you can see

Production plans are only as good as the machines behind them. Capacity shows the weekly load against what each work centre and machine centre can do, so overloads are visible before orders are released.

RolesProduction planner
EditionBusiness Central Premium
StatusPlanned
§ 01 · What it does

Capacity in practice

  • 01

    Weekly load against capacity

    By work centre and machine centre, from planned and firm production orders and from suggestions.

  • 02

    Heat map with drill-down

    See which weeks are overloaded, then open the orders that make up the load.

  • 03

    Level the load

    Move orders earlier or later to even it out, then firm them.

  • 04

    Honest about its limits

    This is rough-cut load planning. Finite sequencing of individual jobs is not part of the first version.

§ 02 · In a real situation

Three orders, one machine

Example

Three production orders all need the same packing line in the week of 20 April, and together they need 62 hours of a 40 hour week.

The heat map shows the week in red. The planner opens it, sees the three orders, moves the one with the latest due date back a week, and watches both weeks fall under capacity before firming anything.
